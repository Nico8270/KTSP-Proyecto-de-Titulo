\begin{abstract}
\noindent The $k$-Traveling Salesman Problem ($k$-TSP) generalizes the TSP:
given $n$ cities with a home city and a fixed $k$ ($1<k\le n$), one selects
exactly $k$ cities and orders them into a single closed tour of minimum length.
We compare ten methods on the 76 TSPLIB instances and the short-/long-run time
protocol of Pandiri and Singh (2020), using LKH-3 as the best-known-solution
reference and the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. The greedy cheapest-insertion
heuristic and Tabu Search are the most robust of our methods and are significantly
better than GVNS under both time budgets. \emph{(This is a quick-preview build; the
submission version is \texttt{sn-article.tex} with the Springer template.)}
\end{abstract}
